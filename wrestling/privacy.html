Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 1, 2026  ·  Governing law: State of Tennessee, USA

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains exactly what data we collect, why we collect it, how we protect it, and what rights you have. We keep this plain and specific — no vague language.

1. Who This Policy Covers

This Privacy Policy applies to all visitors and registered users of Suplex Trading Cards (suplextradingcards.com and its GitHub Pages equivalent). "We," "us," and "our" refer to the operators of Suplex Trading Cards. By using this site you agree to the practices described here.

Data we do NOT collect

  • We do not collect payment information (the site is free and has no paid features)
  • We do not use advertising trackers or sell your data to advertisers
  • We do not use Google Analytics or similar third-party analytics services
  • We do not collect your IP address beyond what Supabase logs for security purposes
  • We do not use tracking pixels or cross-site tracking cookies

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your data only for the following purposes:

  • Account management — to create, authenticate, and maintain your account
  • Service delivery — to save, display, and sync your card checklists
  • Security — to detect and prevent unauthorized access or abuse
  • Communication — to send account-related emails only (verification, password reset). We do not send marketing emails.

We do not use your data for profiling, advertising, or any purpose beyond operating this service for you.

4. Local Storage (Guest Users)

If you use the site without creating an account, your checklist data is stored in your browser's localStorage only — it never leaves your device and is not sent to our servers. Clearing your browser data will erase your guest checklist. We have no access to this data.

5. How We Share Your Data

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for their own purposes. We share data only with:

  • Supabase, Inc. — our database and authentication provider (supabase.com). Supabase stores your account information and checklist data on our behalf under a data processing agreement. Supabase is SOC 2 Type II compliant. Their privacy policy is available at supabase.com/privacy.

We may disclose your data if required by law, court order, or to protect the safety or rights of users or the public.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

This site is not directed to children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately at ghooper33@gmail.com and we will delete that information within 5 business days.

Users between 13 and 18 should obtain parental consent before registering.

7. Your Privacy Rights

You have the following rights regardless of where you live. To exercise any of these, email us at ghooper33@gmail.com.

Access Request a copy of all personal data we hold about you.
Correction Request correction of inaccurate personal data.
Deletion Request deletion of your account and all associated data.
Portability Request your checklist data in a machine-readable format (CSV).
Withdraw Consent Stop using the service and request account deletion at any time.
Object Object to any processing you believe is unlawful or unnecessary.

California Residents (CCPA)

California residents have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act: the right to know what personal information is collected and how it's used, the right to opt out of the sale of personal information (we do not sell personal information), and the right to non-discrimination for exercising privacy rights. To submit a request, email ghooper33@gmail.com with "CCPA Request" in the subject line.

EEA / UK Residents (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area or United Kingdom, our legal basis for processing your data is your consent (given at registration) and our legitimate interest in providing and securing the service. You have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority if you believe your rights have been violated.

8. Data Retention

We retain your account and checklist data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your personal data will be removed from active systems within 30 days. Certain anonymized or aggregated data (e.g., total number of users) may be retained indefinitely as it cannot identify you.

9. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data:

  • All data is transmitted over HTTPS (TLS encryption)
  • Passwords are hashed using bcrypt by Supabase — we have no access to plaintext passwords
  • Database access is governed by Row-Level Security (RLS) — authenticated users can only access their own data
  • Authentication tokens are managed by Supabase's secure session infrastructure

No system is 100% secure. In the event of a data breach that affects your personal information, we will notify you by email within 72 hours of becoming aware of the breach, as required by applicable law.

10. Cookies

We do not use advertising cookies or tracking cookies. The only browser storage we use is:

  • localStorage — to store guest checklist data and Supabase authentication session tokens on your device

You can clear this data at any time by clearing your browser's site data for this domain. Note that clearing it will sign you out and remove any unsynced guest checklist data.

11. Links to Other Sites

This site may contain links to external websites (e.g., card manufacturer sites, marketplace links). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any site you visit.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the effective date at the top of this page. For significant changes that affect how we use your personal data, we will notify registered users by email. Continued use of the site after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
